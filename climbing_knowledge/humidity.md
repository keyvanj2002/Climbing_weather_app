# Humidity and Rock Climbing

## General Principles
Humidity influences evaporation, perceived
comfort, condensation and rock drying.

## High Humidity
High humidity may slow evaporation from wet rock.
It can also increase heat stress during
warm weather.

## Low Humidity
Lower humidity may support evaporation,
depending on temperature, wind and sunlight.

## Condensation
Rock surfaces can become damp when their
temperature falls below the local dew point.

## Climbing Recommendations
Consider humidity together with:
- Temperature
- Dew point
- Recent rainfall
- Wind
- Sun exposure

Humidity alone cannot determine whether
rock is dry or safe to climb.
