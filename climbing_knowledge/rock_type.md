# Rock Types and Weather Sensitivity

## Sandstone
Some sandstone formations are vulnerable
to damage when wet or saturated.

Do not assume sandstone is safe immediately
after rainfall stops.

## Granite
Granite is generally less susceptible to
moisture-related weakening than some sandstone.
However, wet granite can be slippery.

## Limestone
Wet limestone may have significantly reduced
friction and can become slippery.

## Important
Rock properties vary between locations.
Regional geological information does not
establish the condition of a particular route.

## Recommendations
When rock type is unknown, avoid making
rock-specific safety claims.
Always consider local climbing guidance.
