# Wind and Rock Climbing

## General Principles
Wind affects climber stability, communication,
belaying comfort and exposure.

## Wind Speed
Moderate wind may improve cooling and drying,
but can make exposed climbing uncomfortable.

## Wind Gusts
Strong gusts can affect balance on exposed
terrain and make rope handling difficult.

## Exposure
Wind effects depend on:
- Terrain and topography
- Cliff exposure
- Wind direction
- Gust strength
- Climbing and belaying position

## Climbing Recommendations
- Evaluate gusts as well as sustained wind.
- Avoid exposed climbing during dangerous gusts.
- Consider whether wind affects communication.
- Do not assume sheltered conditions from
  a regional forecast alone.
