# Weather Safety for Outdoor Climbing

## Thunderstorms
Lightning poses a serious danger on exposed
cliffs and elevated terrain.
Do not recommend outdoor climbing when
thunderstorms threaten the area.

## Bushfire
Check official fire danger ratings,
warnings, restrictions and access closures.
Avoid climbing in areas affected by
dangerous fire conditions.

## Extreme Heat
Consider heat stress, dehydration,
sun exposure and physical exertion.

## Heavy Rain
Rain can reduce friction and affect
access tracks, drainage and rock stability.

## Severe Wind
Strong gusts may create dangerous conditions
on exposed terrain.

## Recommendation Rules
Safety warnings take priority over comfort.
Do not recommend climbing when a relevant
official warning indicates dangerous conditions.

If critical safety information is missing,
state the limitation rather than assuming safety.
