# Temperature and Rock Climbing

## General Principles
Temperature affects climber comfort, endurance,
friction, hydration and heat-related risk.

## Cool Conditions
Cool, dry conditions may improve friction on
some rock surfaces.
Very cold conditions can reduce dexterity.

## Warm Conditions
Warm conditions may increase sweating and
reduce perceived friction.
Direct sunlight can make rock surfaces
significantly hotter than air temperature.

## Hot Conditions
High temperatures increase the risk of
dehydration, heat exhaustion and heatstroke.
Humidity and direct sun can increase heat stress.

## Climbing Recommendations
Consider:
- Air temperature
- Apparent temperature
- Sun exposure
- Humidity
- Wind
- Climbing intensity

Do not use a single universal temperature
threshold to determine climbing suitability.
